## KenoWiki

This wiki centralizes everything you need to get the best possible experience from KenoLab.

You will find guides, policies, support information, technical content, and everything required to use the platform properly.

## Before starting

Take a few minutes to read the main KenoLab policies:

* **[Hint & Assistance Policy]({{< relref "/guide/guidance-politique.md" >}})**

{{< notice warning "Avoid spoilers" >}}
Helping other players is encouraged, but please do not share direct solutions, flags, credentials, or complete attack paths.
{{< /notice >}}

## Learn by doing

Give the labs everything you've got.

Take your time to enumerate, research unfamiliar technologies, test ideas, fail, retry, and understand what you are doing.

The goal is not only to get the flag, but to **learn something useful along the way**.

{{< notice tip "Getting stuck is part of the process" >}}
Do some research, review what you already tried, and ask for a hint if you really need one.
{{< /notice >}}

## Give us feedback

{{<notice note >}} 
KenoLab is constantly evolving, so do not hesitate to share your feedback. 

Bug reports, lab feedback, difficulty balancing, documentation issues, or simply things you enjoyed are all useful to us. 

You can find the available contact methods on the **[Support page]({{< relref "/support/_index.md" >}})**.
