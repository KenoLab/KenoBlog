Need help with KenoLab, found a bug, or just want to discuss one of our labs?
There are several ways to get in touch with us.

## Contact us by email

For any request, you can contact the KenoLab team directly by email:

{{< notice info "Contact" >}}
**[kenolab.contact@proton.me](mailto:kenolab.contact@proton.me)**
{{< /notice >}}

---

## Discord support tickets

Need help with something specific? You can create a **private support ticket** directly on the **[KenoLab Discord server](https://discord.gg/cXaKPSzgUE)**.

{{< notice security >}}
Support tickets are private between you and the KenoLab team. They are the recommended option when your question may contain sensitive information, screenshots, flags, credentials, or details about a challenge.
{{< /notice >}}

---

## Ask the community

KenoLab is also a community.

If you are stuck on a lab, do not hesitate to discuss it with other members and ask for help.
{{< notice tip>}}
Try to guide other players in the right direction instead of directly giving them the solution. A good hint should help someone understand what to investigate next without solving the challenge for them.
{{< /notice >}}

### Avoid spoilers

Please **do not publicly share** Check: **[Assistance policy]({{< relref "/guide/guidance-politique.md" >}})**

{{< notice warning "Spoilers and direct solutions" >}}
Sharing direct answers or spoilers can ruin the experience for other players.

Repeated or intentional violations may result in a **warning**, and serious or repeated abuse may lead to a **ban from the KenoLab community**.
{{< /notice >}}
